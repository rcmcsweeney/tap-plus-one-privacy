# Privacy Policy for Tap Plus One

Last updated: 3 May 2026

## What we collect

Nothing.

Tap Plus One is a single-player puzzle game that runs entirely on your
device. It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal
information.

## Network

The app does not make any network requests. It works offline and stays
offline.

## Data stored on your device

Your high score for each game mode is stored on your device using the
operating system's standard local-preferences storage. This data never
leaves your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you
uninstall the app.

## Third-party services

None. The app does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics services,
advertising networks, or crash reporters.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect any information,
so it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect data from children
under 13.
